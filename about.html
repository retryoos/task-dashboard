About Argus Defense Systems

Argus Defense Systems is a fictional defense contractor. This site is a task management dashboard for its program teams, built to keep research, procurement and maintenance work organised in one place.

The dashboard lets teams add tasks with due dates and priorities, track their status, filter and sort the list, and see a quick breakdown of what is done and what is still pending.
